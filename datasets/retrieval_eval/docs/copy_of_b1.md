# Silicon anode swelling in lithium cells

Abstract

Silicon anodes store far more lithium than graphite but swell during cycling. We measured silicon anode swelling in thirty pouch cells.

Methods

Each cell was cycled between 3.0 and 4.2 volts at a rate of C/3. Thickness was recorded with a dial gauge after every fifty cycles.

Results

Silicon anode swelling reached 18 percent after two hundred cycles, and capacity fell by 22 percent over the same period. Cells with a carbon coating swelled less than uncoated cells.
