# Electrolyte additives for fast charging

Abstract

Fast charging damages lithium cells unless the electrolyte forms a stable interphase. We tested a fluoroethylene additive in the electrolyte.

Methods

Forty coin cells were charged at six times the nominal current. Half contained two percent fluoroethylene additive by weight.

Results

Cells with the additive kept 91 percent of their capacity after three hundred fast charge cycles, against 74 percent without it. Impedance rose more slowly with the additive present.
