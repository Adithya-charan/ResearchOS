# Biochar and soil water retention

Abstract

Biochar is charred plant matter added to soil. This report tests whether biochar raises the water holding capacity of sandy soil.

Methods

Sandy soil was mixed with zero, two or five percent biochar by weight, wetted to saturation and left to drain.

Results

Water holding capacity rose by 12 percent at two percent biochar and by 25 percent at five percent biochar. Drainage time lengthened with more biochar.
