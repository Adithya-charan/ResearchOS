# Thermal bleaching thresholds on a fringing reef

Abstract

Reef corals expel their algal partners under heat stress. We related bleaching degree to accumulated heating weeks on a fringing reef.

Methods

Sixty colonies were photographed monthly. Bleaching degree was scored from colour charts, and sea temperature came from a logger fixed to the reef.

Results

Bleaching began once accumulated heating weeks passed four, and most colonies were pale at eight heating weeks. Branching colonies bleached earlier than massive colonies.
