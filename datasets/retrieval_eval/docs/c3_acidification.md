# Ocean acidification and skeleton density

Abstract

Seawater that holds more dissolved carbon dioxide lowers the aragonite saturation state. This report links aragonite saturation to coral skeleton density.

Methods

Fragments were grown for ten weeks in tanks held at three levels of aragonite saturation. Skeleton density was measured by weighing in water and in air.

Results

Skeleton density dropped by 9 percent at the lowest aragonite saturation. Growth rate fell by a similar fraction.
