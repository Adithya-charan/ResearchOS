# Larval settlement on reef rubble

Abstract

Coral larvae need a stable surface to settle. We compared larval settlement on loose rubble and on cemented rubble.

Methods

Ceramic tiles were placed on both rubble types for six weeks and then counted under a microscope.

Results

Settlement on cemented rubble was three times higher than on loose rubble. Tiles in shaded crevices collected more larvae than tiles on open surfaces.
