# Mycorrhizal networks and phosphorus uptake

Abstract

Arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi extend the reach of plant roots. We measured phosphorus uptake in maize with and without mycorrhizal hyphae.

Methods

Maize was grown in pots with mesh bags that either admitted or excluded fungal hyphae while keeping roots out.

Results

Plants with access to hyphae took up 40 percent more phosphorus. The benefit disappeared when the soil was fertilised heavily.
