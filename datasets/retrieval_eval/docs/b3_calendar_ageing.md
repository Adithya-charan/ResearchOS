# Calendar ageing of stored lithium cells

Abstract

Lithium cells lose capacity even when idle. This report studies calendar ageing as a function of storage temperature and state of charge.

Methods

Sixty cells were stored for twelve months at 10, 25 and 45 degrees Celsius, at either low or full charge.

Results

Capacity fade after twelve months was 3 percent at 10 degrees, 7 percent at 25 degrees and 19 percent at 45 degrees. Cells stored at full charge faded faster at every temperature.
